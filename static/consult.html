Get a full team consult

Describe your situation once and the whole team looks at it together — research, movement, nutrition, speech and swallowing when relevant, emotional wellbeing, practical help, and stories from other people. You get one plain-language summary with sources, built to print and take to your next appointment.

Just have a question? Ask it in the chat instead — it's faster and only brings in the helpers you actually need.

How this works

  1. You share. A few details about your health, your location, and your language.
  2. The team researches. A medical researcher, a physiotherapist, a dietitian, a speech-and-swallowing specialist (when relevant), an emotional wellbeing specialist, a patient navigator, and a patient-stories helper search trusted public sources — guidelines, published research, patient organizations.
  3. Two more passes. One checks that every organization is named in plain English. One rewrites the whole thing at a reading level anyone can follow, without dropping a single number or source.
  4. You get a summary. Plain language, in your language, with sources you can check. Bring it to your next appointment.
